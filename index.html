ABOUT THE INSTRUCTOR

Encouraging & Patient Teaching Style

My teaching style is patient, encouraging, and highly interactive. I focus on creating a comfortable environment where students feel confident using English.

Every student learns differently. That's why I adapt my lessons to fit your specific needs, whether it's through interactive materials, Callan Reflex exercises, or VOA reading modules. I'm dedicated to helping you achieve fluency, clear pronunciation, and natural grammar skills step-by-step.

Patient & Warm

I explain complex terms gently and celebrate every small achievement.

Interactive lessons

Interactive questions and feedback tools keep classes lively.

Comfortable space

WHAT I TEACH

Programs & Specialties

I offer structured, customized courses to help learners achieve their unique goals.

Kids & Teenagers

Engaging classes for young learners focusing on vocabulary, simple dialogs, and fun phonics exercises.

Beginner to Advanced

Personalized curriculum matching your starting point, scaling smoothly up to high-fluency targets.

Daily Conversational English

Everyday phrases, travel discussion, listening comprehension, and pronunciation tuning.

Business English

Email communication, negotiation language, corporate vocab, and preparation for job interviews.

Callan Method

Dynamic, rapid reflex Q&A classes that train you to reply instantly without translating in your head.

Eiken Preparation

Exam-specific preparation focusing on vocab drills, writing exercises, and mock interview setups.

Also providing custom lessons based on VOA reading exercises and grammar sheets.
