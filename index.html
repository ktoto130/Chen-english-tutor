ABOUT ME

A Patient & Encouraging Guide

For more than seven years, I've taught students from different countries and backgrounds, helping them improve their speaking, listening, reading, and pronunciation skills.

My teaching style is interactive, positive, and encouraging. I focus on creating a comfortable environment where students feel confident using English. Whether you are a young child learning the alphabet or a business professional prepping for a presentation, I customize my sessions to match your pace and interests.

Patient

Perfect for beginners and kids who need a relaxed pace.

Interactive

Engaging conversation topics, VOA news, and quick feedback.

Confidence

Focus on pronunciation and vocabulary to make you feel like a native.

MY SPECIALTIES

Experience & Lesson Programs

Here are some of the specialized programs and audiences I have extensive experience teaching:

Kids & Teenagers

Fun, gamified lessons to keep young minds engaged and build early pronunciation habits.

Beginner to Advanced

Custom coursework suited for absolute beginners or advanced speakers looking to refine nuances.

Daily Conversational English

Real-life dialogue prep, casual interactions, and travel preparation to build effortless speech.

Business English

Email writing, formal vocabulary, job interview training, and workplace culture communication prep.

Callan Method

Fast-paced, question-and-answer reflex system designed to get you thinking directly in English.

Eiken Preparation

Targeted preparation for Eiken examinations, focusing on writing, vocabulary, and mock interviews.

Also offering VOA-based lessons and interactive reading modules.
